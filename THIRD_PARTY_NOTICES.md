The MIT license covers original TraceSplit code and documentation only. It does not relicense dependencies or third-party data.

The engine depends on NumPy, pandas, SciPy, scikit-learn, Lark, and threadpoolctl. Test and reporting utilities use pytest, pytest-cov, coverage, and Matplotlib. Dependencies are obtained from their distributors rather than vendored in this archive. Their installed license notices remain applicable. The included `licenses/scikit-learn-COPYING` is the notice distributed with the installed scikit-learn version used for the dataset loaders and backend.

Five CSV files were exported from the scikit-learn 1.8.0 bundled dataset loaders: iris, wine, breast cancer Wisconsin (diagnostic), optical handwritten digits, and diabetes. The original dataset descriptions are retained as `datasets/*_source.txt`; they identify sources and bibliographic attributions. These files are small research examples, not medical advice or validated deployment data. The diabetes input uses `load_diabetes(scaled=False)`, so fitted normalization is not inherited from the globally scaled loader default. The source descriptions describe the loader generally; the generator records the actual option used here.

The mixed, grouped, and temporal CSV files were generated by `experiments/build_datasets.py` for controlled testing. They are not observations of people and must not be presented as empirical real-world datasets.

The authoritative provenance for this release consists of the generator, source descriptions, dataset hashes in `datasets/manifest.json`, and the scikit-learn dataset documentation.
